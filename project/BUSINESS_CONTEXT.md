# MTL-XF-028 — Business context

## Organisation and pressure

**Local Net Zero Emissions, Sector Decarbonisation & Intervention Prioritisation Intelligence.** A combined authority needs to know which sectors and local authorities contribute most to emissions, where emissions are declining slowly, and where local policy intervention may have the strongest case.

The simulated sponsor, Confidential Combined Authority Net Zero & Programme Investment Board, must decide where to direct limited analytical and service-review attention. Its starting problem is an inconsistent view of local net zero emissions, sector decarbonisation & intervention prioritisation intelligence: totals, rates, periods and categories can lead users to different conclusions without a common definition. The engagement supplies an evidence base; it does not assume the client has already suffered a quantified failure.

## Current process and its consequences

Analysts retrieve official tables independently, prepare local summaries and discuss headline differences in management meetings. Without a shared cohort and definition register, revisions, incomparable denominators and missing coverage can pass into recommendations. The consequence is an investigation shortlist that cannot be explained or reproduced, rather than a verified estimate of financial loss.

## Required decision process

1. Confirm the reporting cohort and available dimensions.
2. Review governed metrics and quality exceptions before interpreting patterns.
3. Compare like-for-like trends and segment evidence against the questions below.
4. Challenge at least two prioritisation scenarios and document changes in shortlisted cases.
5. Assign a responsible role, evidence-gathering action and review point to every proposed investigation.

**Decision:** Which local-authority and sector combinations warrant decarbonisation programme investigation based on emissions scale, trend and realistic local influence?

## Business questions and evidence

| Question | Expected evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Which LA/sector combinations account for the greatest emissions burden? | Reconciled trend/segment table and diagnostic chart with period, grain, denominator and source references. |
| Where are comparable emissions falling slowly or increasing? | Reconciled trend/segment table and diagnostic chart with period, grain, denominator and source references. |
| How does the picture change between territorial and locally influenceable scopes? | Segment or coverage assessment stating which comparisons are supported. |
| Are changes driven by sector mix or shifts within sectors? | Sensitivity or scope audit showing what changes the interpretation. |
| Which additional cost, feasibility and delivery evidence is required for programme investment? | Investigation plan with missing evidence, owner and decision-review trigger. |

## Intended management use

- Build territorial emissions trend and sector contribution bridges, reconciling totals at declared hierarchy.
- Compare 2005 baseline and recent five-year patterns; isolate boundary changes and negative/net emission categories.
- Prioritise up to ten LA/sector investigation cases using burden, trend and a documented local-influence assessment; test at least two weight sets.

The BI executive view starts with the commissioned question, reporting period and coverage. Users inspect a diagnostic view before reading the investigation shortlist. Every case records the signal, its magnitude, uncertainty, alternative explanations, evidence still needed and a next-step owner. The final briefing must explain at least one case where the evidence is insufficient for action.

## Measures of project value

Success means a repeatable review with five supported findings, five reconciled dashboard measures, two compared decision scenarios, ten UAT scenarios and a reproducible handover. Teams may propose a future operational baseline and improvement target, but must label these as proposed measures rather than achieved benefits. No observed savings or service improvement can be claimed from this simulated engagement alone.

## Interpretation and evidence limits

- Territorial emissions and emissions within local influence are different scopes.
- Historical reductions do not prove a local policy caused them; investment recommendations need cost and feasibility evidence.
- Treat negative/net emissions and land-use categories explicitly; no unqualified percentage ranking on nonpositive baselines.

## Dependencies and risks

- Source/schema validation may narrow the scope: record an explicit impact decision rather than inventing fields.
- Data revisions may alter findings: preserve immutable source manifests and rerun comparisons.
- Small, suppressed or non-comparable segments may distort ordering: apply eligibility rules and show excluded cases.
- Local context or operational records may be missing: attach an evidence request to the relevant recommendation.
